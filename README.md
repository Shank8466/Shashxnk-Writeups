## 📝 Writeup Methodology

I aim to document every challenge using a structured penetration testing methodology:

1. **Reconnaissance** – Identify the target's attack surface.
2. **Enumeration** – Discover exposed services, directories, endpoints, and technologies.
3. **Vulnerability Analysis** – Identify potential security weaknesses.
4. **Exploitation** – Validate vulnerabilities in authorized lab environments.
5. **Privilege Escalation** – Investigate opportunities for elevated access.
6. **Post-Exploitation** – Understand the impact and scope of the compromise.

## 📌 Documentation Standards

My writeups aim to provide more than just solutions.

* Explain the underlying vulnerability and its root cause.
* Include relevant commands with appropriate placeholders.
* Explain why a particular technique or approach was used.
* Document failed attempts and troubleshooting where useful.
* Include the security impact and remediation.
* Avoid publishing sensitive information, private keys, credentials, or real-world victim data.
